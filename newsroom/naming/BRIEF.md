# Name brief — Physical AI Magazine

Owner: Brand & Design. Decision: Lior. The working title "מגזין פיזיקל AI / Physical AI Magazine" stays until a name is approved.

## What we need
- Works in Hebrew and English: one word that reads well in both, or a Hebrew name with a natural English counterpart.
- Short (1-2 words), easy to say in both languages, memorable, citable as a masthead.
- Evokes the field (body, motion, mechanism, sensing, embodiment, craft…) without clichés ("RoboNews", "AI Daily", "TechBot").
- Serious, formal-but-warm; fits the "ink & circuit" identity (serif masthead, engineering line drawings, copper accent).
- Clear of conflicts in AI / robotics / media, a usable domain (`.ai` or `.com` or `.co.il`), no live US trademark in classes 9, 16, 35, 38, 41, 42.

## Rejected — do not propose again
Round 1 (23.10.2026, details in `round-1.md`): Lior rejected the whole shortlist as "not good yet".
- Shortlist rejected by Lior: Perek / פרק, Zroa / זרוע, Etzem / עצם, Mifrak / מפרק, Shrir / שריר, Ofan / אופן, Gufa / גופא.
- Rejected in research: Kinema, Motus, Golem / גולם, Ofanim / אופנים, Yetzira / יצירה, Tnua / תנועה, Tzir / ציר, Gid / גידים, Guf / גוף, Maga / מגע, Matter, Atlas, Talos, Servo, Pivot, Hinge, Axon, Kinesis, Torque, Soma, Embodied, Mechanica.

Lesson from round 1: single anatomical Hebrew words felt flat. Try other directions too — compound or coined names, words from craft and engineering, names about the act of recording/witnessing a new era, names that hold up as a brand rather than as a dictionary word.
